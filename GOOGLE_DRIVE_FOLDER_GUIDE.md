## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Locate Your Deliverables Folder on Desktop
All deliverables, complete source code, slide presentations, video animations, audio files, and the ZIP package are waiting in the new folder on your Desktop:
```
C:\Users\robin\OneDrive\Desktop\Dijkstra_RAG_Submission
```
*(Also accessible at `C:\Users\robin\Desktop\Dijkstra_RAG_Submission`)*

### Step 2: Open Google Drive
1. Open your browser and go to: **[https://drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)**
2. Make sure you are logged in to your Google account.

### Step 3: Upload the Complete Folder
1. In Google Drive, click the **+ New** button in the top-left corner.
2. Select **Folder upload**.
3. Navigate to your **Desktop** and select the folder named **`Dijkstra_RAG_Submission`**.
4. Click **Upload** and wait a moment for the upload to finish (~20-30 seconds).

*(Alternatively, you can simply drag the `Dijkstra_RAG_Submission` folder from your Desktop directly into the Google Drive window!)*

### Step 4: Make the Link Shareable & Copy It
1. In Google Drive, find the uploaded **`Dijkstra_RAG_Submission`** folder.
2. Right-click the folder and click **Share** (or click the three dots ⋮ ➔ **Share** ➔ **Share**).
3. Under **General access**, change the setting from **"Restricted"** to:
   👉 **"Anyone with the link"** (Role: **Viewer**).
4. Click the **Copy link** button.
5. Paste this link into the Google Drive Link field on your submission form!
